The lounge

Shisha in Valencia, in the warm light of L'Eixample

Noor means light, and you can feel it here. Copper tiles, sofas made for long nights, columns that glow like embers and a bar where shisha and cocktails speak the same language. A step away from Plaza Cánovas.

For a celebration, a long conversation or nothing in particular. Open every day, from 16:00 to 04:00.

The ritual

A well-made shisha is never rushed

01

Your flavour

Rubio, Fusion, Full Black, Kazan or Josh. Tell us if you like it fresh, sweet, fruity or intense and we will guide you at your table.

02

FAQ

Before you come

What are NOOR Lounge’s opening hours?

We are open every day, Monday to Sunday, from 16:00 to 04:00.

Where is this shisha lounge in Valencia?

At Carrer Serrano Morales, 3, L'Eixample, 46004 València, next to Plaza Cánovas.

How much is a shisha?

Shishas range from €16 (Rubio or Josh) to €30 (Kazan Especial), and €40 for a fresh fruit bowl.

How do I book a table?

Online from this website, for 1 to 10 people: your booking goes straight into our table system. For groups of more than 10, call us on +34 963 12 07 54.

Do you serve cocktails?

Yes: cocktails from €12, including strawberry, mango or passion fruit mojitos, Espresso Martini, PornStar Martini, Negroni and more.

What is the average spend per person?

Between €10 and €20 per person, depending on what you order.
